Online Gambling in Australia

Online gambling in Australia is regulated under the Interactive Gambling Act 2001. Domestic operators are limited, but Australian players widely access international platforms licensed offshore (Curacao, MGA, PAGCOR etc.). These sites offer real-money pokies, live dealer games, poker and extensive sports betting — especially AFL and NRL.

Legal Status in Australia

Online-gambling rules in Australia depend on local law, product type, and operator licensing. Check current regulator or government guidance before registering. An offshore licence alone does not confirm that a site may legally serve residents.

Australian players favor pokies (online slots), blackjack, roulette, live dealer tables and poker. Sports betting focuses on AFL, NRL, cricket, horse racing and football (A-League, EPL). Live in-play betting and cash-out are widely available.
