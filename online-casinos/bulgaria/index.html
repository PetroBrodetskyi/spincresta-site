Online gambling in Bulgaria is fully legal and regulated. Bulgarian players have access to a mature market of NRA-licensed casinos and sportsbooks offering slots, live dealer games, poker and extensive betting markets — especially football.

Regulation and Licensing in Bulgaria

The National Revenue Agency (NRA) oversees online gambling under the Gambling Act. Licensed operators must meet strict standards on fairness, player protection, AML/CFT and responsible gambling. Choosing an NRA-licensed platform ensures full legal protection and high safety standards.
