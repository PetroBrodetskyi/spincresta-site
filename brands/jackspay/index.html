Casino & games

The catalog groups slots, jackpots, crash games, table games, video poker, specialty games, and JacksPay Originals. The slot selection includes Eclipse of Ra and Mythic Wolf. Fresh Deck opens in a separate tab after sign-in and lists live blackjack, poker, and roulette.

Rewards & VIP

The first deposit unlocks Club Member status in Jack’s Royal Club. The club lists weekly deposits for Executive Member status. High Roller and High Roller Elite are invitation-only, with monthly deposit minimums of $5,000 and $10,000, respectively. A VIP host sets withdrawal limits for the higher tiers.

The 5% weekly cashback covers eligible net losses and requires a deposit during the previous week. Jacks Pay credits it on Monday, and players have three days to claim it. The crypto deposit rebate has separate terms.
