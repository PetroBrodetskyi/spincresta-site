How our editorial process works

Our reviews focus on information a player can verify before opening an account or making a deposit.

    We start with the operator information a player can verify before registration: ownership and license disclosures, country restrictions, bonus rules, payment methods, withdrawal conditions, identity checks, support channels, and safer-play controls.   • How we keep information current

    Casino terms and regional availability can change. Pages show an editorial review date where appropriate, and material changes are incorporated during scheduled reviews.
