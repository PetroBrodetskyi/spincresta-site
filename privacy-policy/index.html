How we handle information

The sections below explain our data practices in practical terms, including analytics, service providers, security, transfers, and your rights.

1. Introduction

SpinCresta.com ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit spincresta.com, how we use, share and protect it, and what rights you have regarding your data.

By using our site, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect limited data, mostly automatically:

  • Technical & Usage Data - IP address, browser type/version, operating system, device type, pages visited, time/date of visit, time spent on pages, referring/exit pages, clickstream data
  • Cookies & Similar Technologies - small files that help us remember preferences, analyze traffic and improve site performance. We use essential cookies (for functionality) and analytics cookies (e.g., Google Analytics). You can manage/disable cookies via browser settings
  • Contact Information - name, email and message if you voluntarily contact us via form or email
  • No Sensitive Data - we do not collect financial details, payment info, health data or other sensitive personal information

3. How We Use Your Information

We use data only for legitimate purposes:

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website
  • To analyze usage trends and optimize content/user experience
  • To respond to your inquiries and provide support
  • To detect/prevent technical issues, errors or malicious activity (e.g., spam, bots)
  • To comply with legal obligations, court orders or protect rights/safety

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. Sharing occurs only in limited cases:

  • Service Providers - with analytics (Google Analytics), hosting or security providers under strict confidentiality agreements
  • Legal Requirements - when required by law, subpoena, court order or government request
  • Protection - to protect rights, property or safety of SpinCresta, users or the public
  • Business Transfers - in case of merger, acquisition or sale of assets (with notice where required)

5. Browser Storage and Google Analytics

We store your selected language and display preferences in your browser's local storage so they remain available on your next visit. These values stay on your device until you clear them.

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_... and process session statistics, approximate location, browser and device details, and page interactions. Do not send us sensitive information through URLs or email.

5A. Email Updates and Subscriptions

If you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address only to send occasional SpinCresta updates about new casino reviews, country guides and important availability changes. Subscribing is voluntary and based on your consent.

We use Brevo as our email delivery and contact-management provider. Your email address is sent to Brevo for this purpose. We use double opt-in: after submitting the form, you must confirm your subscription through the link in the confirmation email before it becomes active.

Every update includes an unsubscribe link, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. We retain your address while you are subscribed and may keep a limited suppression record after you unsubscribe so that we do not contact you again.

6. Data Retention

We keep contact correspondence only as long as needed to answer the request, maintain appropriate records, or meet legal obligations. Analytics and technical data are retained according to our configured settings and applicable provider controls, then deleted or aggregated. Retention periods may vary by data type and legal requirement.

7. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location (GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access - request what data we hold about you
  • Correction - ask to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion - request erasure of your data (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Opt-Out - object to processing or restrict it (e.g., marketing, profiling)
  • Data Portability - receive your data in structured format
  • Withdraw Consent - where processing is consent-based

To exercise these rights, email us at affiliates@armadaapp.com. We respond within the period required by applicable law. GDPR requests are normally answered within one month; other jurisdictions may allow a different period. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (HTTPS encryption, access controls, regular security audits) to protect your data. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure - use the site at your own risk.

9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed or stored outside your country (e.g., US, EU servers). We use appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses, adequacy decisions) to ensure protection.

10. Children's Privacy

Our site is not intended for persons under 18 (or 21 in some jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we learn we have such data, we delete it immediately.

11. External Links, Affiliate Tracking, and Infrastructure

SpinCresta contains links to casino and betting operators. A link may include a campaign or partner identifier so the operator can attribute the referral. Once you leave SpinCresta, the operator's privacy policy applies. Ordinary referral links do not give us your casino password, deposits, payment-card details, or gaming history.

Images and site files may be delivered by infrastructure providers such as Cloudinary and Vercel, which may receive standard request data needed to deliver and secure their services.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes may be notified via email or site notice.
